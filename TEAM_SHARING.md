# Team Sharing Guide — CIS 440 Workout Tracker

This guide helps original teammates describe the shared academic project accurately while keeping each person's contribution claims separate.

## Neutral project description

> A six-person CIS 440 academic team designed and implemented a browser-based workout-tracking prototype for beginners. The project connected exercise discovery, workout logging, scheduling, routine planning, progress summaries, privacy-aware community features, and optional calendar integration using JavaScript, Node.js, Express, MySQL, HTML/CSS, and Scrum.

Use or adapt that description only if it accurately reflects your experience. Do not present this repository as an official statement from the full team, the instructor, Arizona State University, or W. P. Carey.

## Contribution accuracy

- Replace every placeholder with your actual role and work.
- Do not copy Jonathan's verified-contribution section unless you genuinely shared that specific work.
- Distinguish your contribution from shared backend, database, authentication, design, testing, integration, and product work.
- Do not use commit count as a substitute for explaining contribution value.
- Do not claim production deployment, active users, adoption, revenue, retention, clinical effectiveness, complete security, live provider validation, or accessibility conformance without your own evidence.
- Do not name or tag another teammate without that person's preference or permission.

## Corrections, naming, role changes, or removal

This repository is Jonathan Rodriguez's case-study presentation, not an official account of every teammate's contribution. An original teammate may request:

- correction of shared project context;
- a preferred public name or continued omission;
- adjustment of a role description;
- clarification of shared versus individual work; or
- removal of material that creates a substantiated privacy or attribution concern.

Use the repository's GitHub issue channel or contact the maintainer through the [Jonathangu4z GitHub profile](https://github.com/Jonathangu4z). Keep credentials, private source, and sensitive evidence out of public issues; use a private contact route when supporting material is sensitive.

Sharing this project is optional. Linking to or reusing the neutral description does not imply endorsement of this repository or agreement with every statement in it.
