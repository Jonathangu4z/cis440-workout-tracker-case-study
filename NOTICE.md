# Notice — Workout Tracker Technical Case Study

## Purpose and independent-portfolio status

This repository is a code-free technical case study of a six-person CIS 440 academic team project. It is maintained by Jonathan Rodriguez as an independent portfolio presentation. It is not an official publication or endorsement by the full team, the instructor, Arizona State University, or W. P. Carey.

The maintainer has not collected or published every original contributor's individual naming preference, so other contributors are not named here. This omission does not minimize their work or convert the project into a sole-authored project.

## Team origin and role-only attribution

The underlying workout-tracking application was team-developed. The public presentation uses role-only context for Scrum Master, Product Owner, database/ERD, frontend/UI, frontend/routing, and backend/API responsibilities. Role labels describe collaboration context; they do not imply exclusive ownership of every feature, file, or decision.

Jonathan Rodriguez is identified as the case-study maintainer and a Frontend / Routing Contributor. His public contribution statements are limited to areas supported by a private evidence review:

- interface and internal-page foundations;
- routing and client-side navigation behavior;
- workout-entry and calendar prototypes;
- responsive exercise-catalog behavior;
- beginner routine planning;
- recovery/reminder heuristics;
- configurable workout-history analytics and chart views; and
- selected naming, browser, routing, and server-startup maintenance.

Shared backend, database, authentication, design, testing, integration, and product work remain attributed to the team unless a narrower statement is supported.
